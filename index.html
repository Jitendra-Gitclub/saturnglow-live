Brand Manifesto

The SaturnGlow Dossier

1. The 240 GSM French Terry Standard

Streetwear is defined by weight and drape. We do not use lightweight poly-blends. Our drop-shoulder tees are built on dense 240 GSM 100% combed cotton French Terry with Lycra reinforced collars to resist neck sagging through wash cycles.

2. Single-Scan Retail Control

Every piece carries a serialized barcode linked to Balotra, Jodhpur, or Barmer allocations. Retail stores scan and lock the warranty at counter checkout, activating 45-day replacement protection across all locations.

3. Cross-Store Replacement Guarantee

Every piece comes backed with our 45-day stitching defect replacement and 21-day color fastness guarantee, exchangeable across any authorized official partner store across India.
